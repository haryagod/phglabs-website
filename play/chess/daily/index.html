Why a daily puzzle?

Getting better at chess is mostly about steady practice. Ten focused minutes every day beats a three-hour session once a month. The daily puzzle gives you one good tactic to think about, and the streak helps you build the habit.

Everyone gets the same puzzle on the same day, so you can challenge friends and family. Did they solve it without a mistake?

A new puzzle appears at midnight your time. Puzzles are from the Lichess puzzle database (CC0).
