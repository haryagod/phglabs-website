How to play

  • Memorise the number before the bar runs out.
  • Type it exactly and press Enter.
  • Correct: the next number is one digit longer.
  • You have two lives. Your score is the longest number you remembered.

What is digit span?

The longest number you can repeat back is called your digit span. It's a classic measure of short-term (working) memory, the kind you use to hold a phone number, an OTP or a sum in your head. Most adults remember about 7 digits, and anything from 5 to 9 is normal.

Tricks to remember more digits

  • Chunking: read 4 9 1 7 2 0 3 as "491 · 720 · 3". Three chunks are far easier than seven separate digits. This is why phone numbers are written in groups.
  • Say it in your head: silently repeating the number keeps it fresh until you type it.
  • Find patterns: dates, ages, pin codes or a rise-and-fall rhythm make digits stick.
