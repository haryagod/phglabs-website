How to play

  • Tap the box (or press Space) to start.
  • It turns red: wait…
  • When it turns green, tap as fast as you can.
  • Tapping too early doesn't count, so you'll redo that try.

What is a good reaction time?

For a visual signal like this one, most people react in roughly 200 to 300 milliseconds. Under 200 ms is very quick, and well-trained gamers and athletes often reach that. On a phone, add a little extra time for the touch screen itself.

Your result changes with how alert you are. Sleep, caffeine, warming up and even the device you use all make a difference. Try it in the morning and again at night, and compare.

Can you improve your reflexes?

Yes, up to a point. Practice makes your response more consistent, and sports, music and action games all train quick reactions. Good sleep matters most: tiredness slows you down more than anything else.
