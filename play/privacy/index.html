Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what information PHG Labs Play (play.phglabs.in), run by PHG Labs, Karad, Maharashtra, India, collects and how it is used.

No accounts, no personal details

You can use every game without signing up. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Scores saved on your device

Your best scores, streaks, settings and saved chess games are stored in your own browser using local storage. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for play.phglabs.in.

Server logs

Like almost every website, our server keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to keep the site secure and working. We don't use them to identify you, and they are deleted automatically.

Advertising and cookies

To keep the site free, we may show ads from Google AdSense. When ads are shown:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Fonts

The site uses fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google's servers.

Children

The games are suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
