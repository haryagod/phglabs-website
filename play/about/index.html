About PHG Labs Play

PHG Labs Play is a free collection of games that make you think: coding challenges, logic puzzles, quick brain tests and chess. Everything runs in your browser, there's nothing to install, and you never need an account.

Who makes it

We're PHG Labs, a software company in Karad, Maharashtra, India. We build business software such as AyurOS for Ayurvedic clinics and Shop Stock for retail shops, along with custom web and mobile apps. We made this site because we enjoy puzzles as much as code, and we think learning should feel like playing.

What you'll find here

  • Code games: Output Guess (predict what JavaScript prints) and Bug Hunt (find the one broken line). Every answer comes with an explanation.
  • Puzzles: Sudoku in three levels and Number Series for aptitude-test practice.
  • Brain tests: the Reflex Test for reaction time and Digit Memory for short-term memory.
  • Chess: a daily puzzle, a puzzle trainer with 2,400 positions from real games, and a full game against the computer.

We write every question, explanation and guide ourselves, and we check the code games automatically so every answer is correct.

What we believe

  • Free for everyone. No paywalls and no sign-up.
  • Respect your time. Short games that teach you something real.
  • Respect your privacy. Scores are saved on your own device, not on our servers. See our privacy policy.
