Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information PHG Labs (“we”), a software company in Karad, Maharashtra, India, collects through this website, phglabs.in, and our free games site, play.phglabs.in, and how it is used.

Enquiries

The enquiry form on this site does not store anything on our servers. When you press Send on WhatsApp, your browser opens WhatsApp with your message filled in, and you choose whether to send it. If you contact us, we use your name, phone number and message only to reply to you and to discuss the product or service you asked about. We never sell your details.

Server logs

Like almost every website, our server keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to keep the site secure and working. We do not use them to identify you.

To keep our games free, we show ads from Google AdSense on play.phglabs.in. Google may also load its AdSense code on this site.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors’ cookies at aboutads.info.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Games and saved scores

The games on play.phglabs.in need no account. Your scores, streaks and settings are stored only in your own browser (local storage) and are never sent to us. See the games privacy policy for details.

Fonts

Our sites use fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google’s servers.
